# Vocabulary Map + Title Proposal — For Sign-off

Petromac website vocabulary, search mapping, and meta title/description standards.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy

- **Goal**: Transition from brand-only search traffic to category, tool-compatibility, and operational outcome search traffic.
- **Title Budget**: All full titles must remain $\le 60$ characters. Suffix ` | Petromac` (11 chars) is added automatically by the root layout, so un-branded base titles must be $\le 49$ characters.
- **Spelling Policy**:
  - `-iser` used on family/brand pages (`Focus™ Centralisers`) and UK/NZ-oriented products (`CP8`, `CRIL`, `CX9`).
  - `-izer` used on US-volume weighted products (`CP12`, `CA7`, `CRU`).
  - `catalog` standardized site-wide (never `catalogue`).
